Why this environment matters

Phone calls and texts are where the scheduling problem already happens.

For the client

They can reach a real business immediately, get answers in the same thread, and receive a confirmed quote appointment without installing an app.

For the electrician

They can capture leads and manage changes while their hands and attention stay on the job site. Important actions return as clear SMS confirmations.

ETAi uses the phone as the product surface and Ambiguous as the shared calendar system of record.

etAI

Technical credibility

One scheduling brain connects messages to the Ambiguous calendar.

Client or contractor phone

Texts a quote request, appointment change, or delay notice.

→

Messaging service

Normalizes inbound texts and sends every outbound reply.

→

Scheduling agent

Keeps job state, classifies intent, and composes the next reply.

↔

Ambiguous workspace

Returns availability and stores calendar events plus customer records.

Replies travel back through the messaging layer. Availability is checked before ETAi proposes a time. ETAi records a confirmed appointment only after the client selects a slot.
